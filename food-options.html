Two places in The Hague to keep in mind when you feel hungry after 10 p.m.

Two places to try

El Basha

Laan 8, 2512 GM The Hague

El Basha serves Middle Eastern food. Have a look at the menu if you want to compare its dishes and takeaway options.

  • Check the menu for the food you want.
  • Ask the restaurant about dietary requirements.
  • Call 070 365 7776 if you are arriving late.
El Basha website

McDonald's Buitenhof

Buitenhof 22, 2513 AG The Hague

McDonald's Buitenhof is another option in the centre. Check its current menu and restaurant hours before heading over.

  • Compare individual items and meals.
  • Check which services are available that evening.
  • Call 070 365 0958 if you have a question.
McDonald's website
