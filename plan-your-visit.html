Plan your visit

Think about the journey, waiting time and how you will get home before you leave.

Before leaving

  1. Choose a food spot and check its address.
  2. Check the opening hours for the night you are visiting.
  3. Look up the journey and leave time to wait for your order.
  4. Decide whether to eat in or take away.
  5. Check your return journey if you are using public transport.

Eat in or take away?

Check whether seating is available late at night. A restaurant may only offer takeaway near closing time.

Ask before ordering if you want to sit down.

Allow a little extra time

A 10-minute walk and a 15-minute wait means 25 minutes before you start eating. This is an example, so leave some extra time for a queue.
