Food on a budget

A late meal does not have to cost more than you planned. Check the full price before ordering.

Keep the total in mind

Set a limit

Decide how much you want to spend before looking at the menu. Include your food, drink and any extras.

Compare meal deals

Check whether a drink or side is included. A deal is only useful if you actually want the items in it.

Check delivery fees

Look for delivery costs, service fees and minimum orders. Compare the final total with collecting it yourself.

€10

A simple example

If you set aside €10, you might allow €7 for food, €2 for a drink and €1 for an extra. These are example amounts, not restaurant prices.

Small extras can add up. Check the menu before ordering, and ask for the total if you are unsure what is included.

Use the current menu

Prices can change. Open the restaurant websites to check their current menus.
